## [Unreleased]
- 2026-08-12: **Fixed a 500 on QR scan and QR-bound payment when the same QR was issued twice.** `requireIssuedQr` looked the payload up with `Optional<QrCode> findByQrPayloadString`, but `qr_payload_string` is not unique — the same VPA, merchant name and fixed amount always render to the same `upi://pay?…` string, which is exactly what makes a printed static merchant QR reusable. A second "Generate QR" with unchanged inputs therefore made every later scan and pay on that payload throw `IncorrectResultSizeDataAccessException` (`Query did not return a unique result: 2 results were returned`). The lookup is now `findAllByQrPayloadString` and resolves the live QR among duplicates: a re-issued dynamic QR revalidates the payload, and the payload is only `QR_EXPIRED` once every QR behind it has lapsed. `QR_NOT_RECOGNIZED` and `QR_AMOUNT_MISMATCH` behaviour is unchanged. Added `UpiQrScanTest` (5 tests) covering duplicates, live-beats-expired, all-expired, static QR, and forged payloads.
- 2026-08-12: Built the **Transaction and UPI frontend modules** and the app shell that hosts them — `frontend/src/modules/transaction` (account picker, balance card, deposit/withdraw/transfer, editable `X-Idempotency-Key`, history table) and `frontend/src/modules/upi-notification-audit` (profile create, send money, change PIN, QR generate/scan). Both render the backend's `errorCode` on rejection, since the business rules are only observable through those codes. `App.jsx` gained tab navigation, an acting-customer switcher, and an `/actuator/health` poll; `vite.config.js` proxies `/api` and `/actuator` to `localhost:8080` so the browser stays same-origin. Verified in a browser against the live stack: a UI deposit moved the real balance and returned a reference number, QR generate → scan round-tripped, a hand-edited payload was rejected as `QR_NOT_RECOGNIZED`, and a UPI payment between the two seeded profiles completed.
- 2026-08-12: Fixed stale selections in `UpiWorkspace` — the VPA dropdowns kept the previous customer's VPA when the acting customer changed, which rendered as an empty `<select>` and silently disabled the buttons. Selections are now dropped unless they still belong to the loaded customer.
- 2026-08-12: **Fixed a QR integrity gap found during live smoke testing** (BR-UPI-004). `scanQr` fell back to parsing any `upi://` string it was handed, and `enforceQrIntegrity` no-opped when the payload was not in the QR store — so a hand-crafted or expired payload was accepted, making the 15-minute expiry unenforceable. Both paths now go through `requireIssuedQr`, which rejects unissued payloads (`QR_NOT_RECOGNIZED`) and expired ones (`QR_EXPIRED`). Removed the now-dead `parseQrPayload`/`ParsedQr`.
- 2026-08-12: **Verified the Transaction and UPI modules end-to-end against the live stack** — Flyway V1+V2 applied to Postgres 16.14, `ddl-auto: validate` passed, `Tests run: 46, Failures: 0`, and an HTTP smoke test covering idempotent replay, the ₹50k single-txn cap, the ₹100k daily ceiling at its exact boundary, the 120s duplicate shield, persisted FAILED records, UPI PIN lockout (including that change-PIN is not a brute-force bypass), the 10-txn daily count ceiling, and QR forgery/tamper rejection.
- 2026-08-12: Built Divya's **UPI module** (`com.nextgen.bank.upi`) — `UpiService`/`UpiServiceImpl`, `UpiController` (`/api/v1/upi` profile, pin, pay, qr, qr/scan), `UpiPinAttemptRecorder` (REQUIRES_NEW so PIN lockout survives rollback), and `V2__upi_pin_lockout.sql`. Enforces BR-UPI-001 (`@nextgen` VPA), BR-UPI-002 (BCrypt PIN + 3-strike 24h lock), BR-UPI-003 (₹100k / 10-txn daily), BR-UPI-004 (15-min dynamic QR expiry). Delegates fund movement to the Transaction Engine; emits `UPIPINChangedEvent` and `UPIPaymentCompletedEvent` via the outbox. Added `UpiProfileDomainTest`.

Enforces BR-TXN-001 (idempotency), BR-TXN-002 (₹50k single / ₹100k daily), BR-TXN-004 (120s duplicate shield), BR-TXN-005 (atomic debit/credit).
